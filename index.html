GOOD QUESTION

A few things
you might be wondering.

Still need a hand? Contact the Pikop team and we’ll point you in the right direction.

How do I book a delivery? +

Download Pikop from Google Play, open the app and choose your service. Bookings are made in the app, not on this website.

Where is Pikop available? +

Pikop currently lists Port Harcourt, Abuja and Lagos as service locations. Availability depends on your zone and participating merchants.

How is the delivery fee calculated? +

Fees are zone-based and may vary by city, pickup and destination zones, route and service. Review the final fee in-app before confirming.

Can I pay cash to the rider? +

Cash on Delivery orders are prepaid through Pikop. Riders are not expected to collect cash from customers.

What payment methods are available? +

Depending on availability, payment options include wallet, bank transfer, card and USSD. Check the checkout screen for current options.

Can I schedule a pickup? +

Scheduled pickup options are available where offered in the app. Check the booking screen for available times.

How do I become a merchant or fulfiller? +

Contact the Pikop team using the details below or WhatsApp. Onboarding and verification requirements apply.
